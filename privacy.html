Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Contribution Arc ("the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information:

  • Email address: obtained during sign-in via Google or GitHub.
  • User identifiers: automatically generated account IDs, display names, and profile pictures used to identify your account.
  • User-generated content: text you enter, including posts, replies, daily reports, and workspace recruitment messages.
  • Usage data: data generated through the App's features, such as study session length, categories, and achievement records.
  • Purchase history: transaction information for in-app purchases (e.g. Arc currency) made through the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to operate the App. Specifically:

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain accounts
  • To display posts, recruitments, and daily reports
  • To save and display learning records
  • To process in-app purchases and manage balances
  • To investigate abuse or service issues

We do not use the collected information for advertising, selling data to third parties, or behavioral targeting.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services and share information only to the extent necessary:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, analytics): provided by Google LLC. Data is stored as required to provide the service. Firebase privacy statement
  • Apple In-App Purchase: used for billing through the Mac/iOS App Store. Transactions are processed by Apple Inc. Apple privacy policy

We will not share information with any other third parties except as required by law.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Collected information is stored on Firebase, hosted on Google Cloud Platform. When you delete your account, the data associated with that account is removed within a reasonable period.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request disclosure, correction, or deletion of your data
  • Delete your account
  • Withdraw consent to third-party sharing

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable period after verifying your identity. Account deletion is also available directly inside the App (Settings → Your Data → Delete Account).

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the collected information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. These include strict access controls via Firebase Security Rules, HTTPS for all communication, and secure handling of credentials.

7. Children Under 13

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will promptly delete the account and its data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
