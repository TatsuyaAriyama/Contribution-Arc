Frequently Asked Questions

I want to delete my account

In Settings, tap "Delete account" to remove your account and the data associated with it. This action cannot be undone.

My Arc currency isn't updating

After an in-app purchase, your balance normally updates within a few seconds. If it doesn't appear within a few minutes, please email us with your Apple ID receipt attached.

I can't see my study logs or posts

Check your network connection and quit and relaunch the app. In the PWA, you can also pull down on the feed to refresh. If the issue persists, please contact support.

Friend requests don't seem to arrive

Ask the other side to restart the app as well. Firestore real-time sync is normally instant, but it can be delayed depending on network conditions.
